Weekly rhythm

  • 1 piece of writing: 2 hrs. Press releases, pitches, op-eds, or plain-language explainers of technical topics. Make the first one a launch release for a Chromatic app.
  • The week's task: about 2 hrs.
  • Networking or industry news: 1 hr.

That is 5 hours a week and 120 hours over the 24 weeks.

Flagged omissions and assumptions

  • The plan assumes about 5 hours a week.
  • 24 weeks leaves about 2 weeks of buffer for exams. Add buffer weeks from the Notes and buffer panel in any week, and every later date moves.
  • Paid certifications are left out.
  • Weeks run in order. If one slips, add a buffer week before the next one.
  • Progress is stored in this browser only. Use Export progress to back it up.

Tech skills and priority

Priority order: SQL and analytics first, then the NLP monitoring tool, then security-comms knowledge.

1. SQL and data visualization
W1 to W6. SQL, GA4, and Looker Studio let you prove campaign impact. This is the biggest gap between you and other PR grads.
2. LLMs and NLP for comms
W9 to W12. Extend your Sherlock-style tool into brand monitoring: sentiment trends, topic clustering, and automated media digests. Check AI output against hand-labeled data.
3. OSINT for reputation and misinformation
W13 and W14. Narrative tracking, spotting impersonation, verifying images and sources, with public data only.
4. Cybersecurity literacy for communications
W15 and W16. Incident response, how breaches are reported, Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019, and ODPC breach notification.
5. Web and SEO fundamentals
W7 and W8, with analytics tagging in W1. Your portfolio and Chromatic sites are the testing ground.
6. Martech and automation
W17 to W19. CRM and email tools like HubSpot. Not scheduled: scheduling and workflow tools. Try them during the W19 launch.

Where the first skills list lands

Strategic writing
The weekly writing piece, every week.
Comms analytics
Months 1 to 3: GA4, SQL, Looker Studio, and your media monitoring tool.
AI and automation workflows
Month 3: the NLP monitoring tool with sentiment and topic clustering.
Crisis and reputation management
Month 4: verification, narrative tracking, and the W16 playbook and tabletop drill.
Content production
SEO in W7 and W8, newsletter in W17, short-form video in W19. Not scheduled: Figma or Canva. Use one of them for the W22 press kit.
Community and social strategy
W19 and W20: posts and engagement metrics. Not scheduled: platform algorithms and influencer partnerships. Fold them into W18 planning.
Presenting and stakeholder skills
W24 mock pitch, the W2 LinkedIn rewrite, and the weekly networking hour.
Niche: tech and cybersecurity communications
Month 4. Companies struggle to explain technical products and incidents to the public, and your dual background fits.
Biggest accelerator: your own projects as PR case studies
W18 to W22: launch plan, press kit, and results report for a Chromatic release, then into your portfolio. A real campaign with measured outcomes beats a certificate.
Internships
Don't wait for W23. Use the weekly networking hour to start conversations early with agencies, startups, and comms teams. Nairobi's tech scene is a good place for that.
